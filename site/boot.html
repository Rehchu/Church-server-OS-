Arise Server · what the box itself shows

From power-on to the desktop.

This is the OptiPlex's own screen in the closet, scene by scene, with sample data: the one-time boot menu, the unattended Ubuntu install, the Arise installer, the console banner, a login, the arisetui dashboard, and finally the Arise desktop the four admins use, full screen on the monitor itself with --kiosk. Staff never see any of this: they sign in on their Macs as normal. Press Enter or the Next button to move through it; in the dashboard scene, keys 1 to 5 switch tabs.

What is real here
Every screen is a thing in the repo: installer/autoinstall.yaml, installer/install.sh, config/console/issue, the login message, cli/arisetui and arise-console.service. The numbers are sample data.

    
What is not
The boot menu is a stand-in for Dell's; the install log is condensed from minutes to seconds; nothing here touched a real domain or NAS yet.

    
The closet monitor
With install.sh --kiosk, tty1 shows the Arise desktop all day (arise-kiosk.service: cage + Firefox, locks itself after ten idle minutes). Each admin who signs in gets their own Firefox profile; locking puts the monitor back on a neutral one. --console gives the text arisetui instead. Alt+F2 is a normal login either way.
