# Friday at the church: what to grab

Photos are fine for all of it. Nothing on this list changes a setting.

## The Dell (questions 1 to 3)
- [ ] The model sticker (e.g. "OptiPlex 7050 SFF") and the service tag.
- [ ] RAM and drives: boot it once, F2 at the Dell logo, read System Information. Or the sticker inside the lid.
- [ ] Is there a free drive bay or M.2 slot? One look inside.

## The Synology (questions 4 to 7)
- [ ] Model (the sticker on the back) and how many bays are filled.
- [ ] Which box its cable goes to: the switch, the office Netgear, or the Mesh.
- [ ] DSM: Storage Manager page (RAID type, size, free), and whether the admin password is known.
- [ ] Its address (Control Panel > Network).

## The routers (exact addresses)
- [ ] Netgear A (at the modem): its LAN address and DHCP range (the "LAN setup" page). Thought to be 192.168.0.1 or 10.0.0.1.
- [ ] Netgear B (office): confirm 10.0.0.1 and its DHCP range; the printer's address.
- [ ] Google Mesh: the Google Home app > Wi-Fi > Network settings > Advanced > LAN shows its range.
- [ ] Which port of each Netgear goes to the switch: the Internet/WAN port or a LAN port.

## The switches
- [ ] The main switch's label: brand, model, and whether it says PoE / 802.3af / 802.3at.
- [ ] Do the port LEDs light for the camera cables?
- [ ] The cameras' switch behind the Mesh: label, PoE or not.

## The security cameras
- [ ] Brand and model on one camera, and whether its own LED is on.
- [ ] Did they come with a recorder (NVR)? If so, where is it plugged in?
- [ ] Any sticker or card with the installer's default address or login.

## Power and the closet
- [ ] The UPS model (for auto-shutdown).
- [ ] A free wired port by the closet for the server.

## While you are there
- [ ] Count the Macs, note the oldest macOS version, and which Mac runs Proclaim.
- [ ] The list of department accounts (Children's Department, Inedge, Media, ...).
