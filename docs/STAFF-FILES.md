# Your files at Arise

This is for staff and volunteers. It is short on purpose.

## On a church Mac

- Sign in with your church login: **first.last** and your password.
- Your **Desktop** and **Documents** are kept on the church server as well as on the Mac. Save
  things there as you always do. They are on every church Mac you sign in to, and they are
  backed up every night.
- The church folders are in Finder's sidebar under **Nextcloud**: Church, your department,
  Shared with me. They open when you double-click them; a small cloud means "not downloaded
  yet", and that is fine.
- The **first time**, the Mac may ask whether Nextcloud may use your Desktop and Documents
  folders. Click **Allow**.
- The small cloud icon at the top of the screen is Nextcloud. Click it to see whether
  everything is up to date.

## If two copies of a file show up

That happens when the same file was changed in two places at once. You do not need to do
anything: within the hour the server keeps the newer one under the real name and puts the
older one in that folder's Trash. If the older one was the one you wanted, ask Bradly or
Kristina; nothing is lost.

## Deleting

Something you delete goes to the Trash first and stays there for 30 days.

## Space

Each person has 50 GB. Nothing ever stops working when you pass it: you get a message at 80% and
at 95%, and so does Bradly. Earlier versions of your files are kept for a year.

## Sending a file to someone outside the church

Ask Bradly or another admin: they can make a link that works for 7 days. For people at church,
use **Share with…** instead.

## The church contacts

Everyone's details are in **Contacts** on a church Mac and on your phone, read-only, as an
account called **Arise Church**: open `https://arise-server.ad.arisecenla.com/contacts.mobileconfig`
on the Mac or iPhone, approve it in Settings, and type your church login. Off church Wi-Fi the
phone needs Tailscale on. To change someone's details, ask Bradly.

## On your phone

0. Once per phone, Bradly or Kristina puts the **church certificate** on it (so the phone
   trusts the church's own address). On an iPhone that is two steps: install it, then turn it on
   in Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
1. Install the **Nextcloud** app (App Store or Google Play).
2. When it asks for a server, type: `https://cloud.ad.arisecenla.com`
3. Bradly or Kristina gives you a **device password** for your phone. On the sign-in page, tap
   **Log in with an app password** (or "alternative log in"), then type your church login
   (first.last) and that device password. Not your church password.
4. Away from church, the phone needs **Tailscale** switched on to reach it.

The phone shows your files and the church folders. That is all it does.

## In a web browser

The same address, `https://cloud.ad.arisecenla.com`, works in a browser on the church network
(or with Tailscale on). Sign in with your church login.

## Lost a phone or a Mac?

Tell Bradly or Kristina straight away. They can cut that one device off; your files stay safe.
You get a message when a device is cut off, and when your account signs in from a new device.
