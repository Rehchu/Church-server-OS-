# The portal guide

This is the page members see in the Help app. It is short on purpose: the
portal is the server's desktop with only your own things in it.

## Signing in

Open the dashboard address the media team gave you (on the church network it
is `http://arise-server:4147`; away from the building it needs Tailscale on
first). Sign in with your church name, written `first.last`, and the same
password you use on a church Mac. One password for the Macs, the shares and
this page.

Forgotten it? Ask the media team; an administrator resets it. Nobody else can
see it.

## Your files

Finder opens on **My files**, your home folder on the server. The shared
folders your groups allow sit under it. Drag a file onto a folder to upload
it, or press **Upload**. Double-click to open; right-click for more. What you
save on a church Mac is here too, and what you put here appears on the Mac.

A deleted file goes to the Trash for 30 days before it is gone. Right-click a
file and pick **Previous Versions** to get last night's copy back.

## Share links

Right-click any file or folder and pick **Share Link…**. Anyone with the link
can download it for seven days, no sign-in needed. **Shared Links…** in the
flame menu lists yours; remove one there when it has done its job.

## The Sunday list

The **Sunday** app has the service checklist and the countdown to the next
service. Anyone signed in may tick an item; it shows who ticked it and when.
The list clears itself each week.

## The calendar

The **Calendar** app has the services and the church's events. Press
**Subscribe** to put it in the Calendar app on your Mac or phone; it keeps
itself up to date.

## The phone app

On a phone, open the dashboard in the browser and choose **Add to Home
Screen** (Safari: the share button; Chrome: the three dots). It then opens
like an app. Alerts go to the administrator only.

## Two-step sign-in

Two-step sign-in is optional. If you turn it on, from the flame menu, you
scan a code once with **Microsoft Authenticator** and type the six digits it
shows when you sign in. Nobody is made to turn it on.

## Help, and who to ask

**Help** (this app, or F1) has this guide. Press **Show me around** in
Control Center for the five-step tour again. For anything else, the media
team: they run the server.
