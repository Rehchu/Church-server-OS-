# What's new in ariseOS

The top section is what the About window and the morning report show after an
update: ten lines at most, in plain words. Older sections stay for the record.
Releases are named after books of the Bible from v0.7 on (round 2, 20).

## v0.8 "Exodus" (in progress): the everyday OS and the safety basics

- A church Calendar everyone can subscribe to, with a Weather widget for Pineville
- Trash and Previous Versions in Finder, plus a text editor, Compress, Get Info, Recents
- System Settings in one window, Software Update, the Store with the 24 apps preinstalled
- Activity Monitor, Logs, Automations and Storage for the administrators
- Help inside the dashboard, Calculator and Clock, snap windows, accessibility switches
- Two-step sign-in is optional; Microsoft Authenticator when you turn it on
- A break-glass admin, disk encryption unlocked by the TPM, a snapshot before every update
- A weekly virus scan of the shares and the church certificate on every Mac
- Restart and Shut Down in the flame menu, never during a service

## v0.7 "Genesis": the feel

- The name ariseOS; About shows the week
- The Bone light theme beside House Lights Down
- Quiet sounds, off by default
- The first-sign-in walkthrough
- The phone app (install to a home screen) and push to Bradly only
- The boot splash with no sound, UPS shutdown, the monthly restore drill, the closet card

## v0.6: Claude on the box

- Claude Code installed by the installer, signed in with the church's account
- The Assistants app: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, YouTube Studio, StreamYard
- The Sunday app, the verse of the day, the screensaver and the lobby sign
- The Mail app: the church family list and newsletters through Resend
- Care an old PC needs: unattended security upgrades, SMART, journald caps

## v0.5: everything installed

- One install brings up eleven apps with generated passwords, Cockpit and Tailscale
- Staging mode for installing at home without touching the house
- The Network app: everything on the switch, camera discovery, the office router's list
- No router changes, ever: the server pins its own address

## v0.4: everyone signs in

- The member portal: your files, the shares your groups allow, share links
- Mac auto-enrol and home sync; existing Mac accounts map to first.last
- Fifty-two answers recorded in docs/ANSWERS.md

## v0.3: the shell

- Finder, menu bar, Spotlight, Mission Control, widgets, Control Center, the lock screen
- The desktop on the closet monitor (kiosk on tty1)

## v0.2: the desktop and remote access

- Windows, a floating dock, Launchpad; Sermons, Bible, Terminal, Files
- Tailscale and WireGuard, Guacamole, the three campuses

## v0.1: the server

- The directory (Samba AD DC), files, the automator, backups, apps, the Synology
- The dashboard, arisectl, the installers, the docs
