# Start here

Welcome to Arise Church's server. This folder is yours: nobody else in the
church can open it (the administrators can, and every time they do it is
written down).

## Where your files go

- **Desktop**: what you are working on now.
- **Documents**: everything you keep: letters, plans, spreadsheets.
- **Photos**, **Videos**, **Music**: what the names say.
- **Downloads**: what you download; tidy it now and then.

The **Church** folder is shared with everyone, and your department has its own
folder. Put things there when other people need them.

## On your Mac

Your Mac keeps these folders in step with the server by itself. Save as you
always do; it reaches the server, and from the server the nightly backup.
Something deleted goes to the Trash for 30 days first.

## From home

Install **Tailscale** on your laptop and sign in with the church's account
(Bradly sets it up with you). Then your files open from home as they do at the
church.
